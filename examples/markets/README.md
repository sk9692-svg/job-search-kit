# Example Markets rows

Starting points for the tracker's **Markets** tab. Copy the rows you need and adjust keywords and cities to your field.

**Visa and immigration rules change often. Check the official government sites before relying on any of this.**
This is not legal or immigration advice.

| Country | Work-rights route (if you need sponsorship) | Job boards | Search keywords | Cities | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Canada | Employer-supported work permit: LMIA, or LMIA-exempt routes such as the Global Talent Stream. The government's Positive LMIA Employers List (open.canada.ca) shows who has sponsored before. | Job Bank, LinkedIn alerts, Indeed (verify on employer site), company careers pages | {your role keywords} | Toronto, Ottawa, Montreal, Vancouver, Calgary | |
| Germany | EU Blue Card for qualified non-EU workers. Look for English-language roles and "relocation" or "visa support" in ads. | StepStone, Arbeitnow, Berlin Startup Jobs, LinkedIn alerts, company careers pages | {your role keywords} English | Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt | Reject roles requiring fluent German unless you have it. |
| New Zealand | Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV): the employer must be accredited by Immigration NZ (public list available). | Seek NZ (alerts by email), LinkedIn alerts, company careers pages | {your role keywords} | Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch | Alerts sender: from:seek.co.nz |
| Australia | Employer-sponsored visas (e.g. Skills in Demand, subclass 482) through an approved sponsor. | Seek AU (alerts by email), LinkedIn alerts, company careers pages | {your role keywords} | Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane | Reject ads requiring citizenship, PR or "full work rights". Alerts sender: from:seek.com.au |
| United Kingdom | Skilled Worker visa through a licensed sponsor (the Home Office publishes the register of licensed sponsors). | LinkedIn alerts, Otta/Welcome to the Jungle, company careers pages | {your role keywords} | London, Manchester, Edinburgh | |

If you don't need sponsorship in a country, write "Citizen / no sponsorship needed" in the route column. The agent will
then skip the sponsorship check and focus on fit.
